Local, monochrome export identifiers. All SVGs are served from public/ and rendered
as CSS masks, using the same theme color and 28px (mobile 24px) square icon boxes.
Lucide remains the navigation/action icon library; no JavaScript dependency added.

godot.svg: official Godot mascot, monochrome press-kit SVG.
  https://godotengine.org/press/
  https://godotengine.org/assets/press/icon_monochrome_dark.svg
  Andrea Calabro, 2017; CC BY 4.0. Added viewBox and currentColor only.
unity.svg: Simple Icons Unity cube, retrieved through Better Icons / Iconify.
  https://api.iconify.design/simple-icons/unity.svg
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0)
gdevelop.svg: official site's monochrome pinned-tab icon.
  https://gdevelop.io/favicons/safari-pinned-tab.svg
  Removed XML/metadata and replaced black with currentColor; geometry unchanged.
aseprite.svg: locally drawn, smooth face identifier, referencing Aseprite's
  rounded rectangular face, two eyes and lower edge. Curved SVG paths replace the
  pixel staircase of the original/Simple Icons mark. Not an official brand asset.
  Reference: https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/data/icons/ase128.png
phaser.svg: locally drawn angular P monogram, referencing the geometric lettering
  of Phaser's wordmark. Not an official Phaser logo. The research found no suitable
  official square monochrome SVG; the full wordmark becomes unreadable at 24/28px,
  the planet logo is an illustration, and the old spaceship favicon is pixel art.
  https://phaser.io/download/logo
  https://cdn.phaser.io/images/logo/phaser-logo.svg
  https://phaser.io/download/trademark
generic.svg: lucide:image (ISC), retained for PNG + JSON.
  https://lucide.dev/
