PixelDoll privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026. Applies to the PixelDoll app at https://pixeldoll.app/.

PixelDoll is a free, open-source app that runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer does not receive your characters, your artwork or your PixelLab key.

What stays on your device

Your characters, artwork, settings and drafts are saved in your browser's storage on this device. If you choose to remember your PixelLab key, it is saved there too. Clearing your browser's data for pixeldoll.app, or Settings › Library › Delete local work, removes them.

PixelLab

When you generate artwork, your browser sends your request and your PixelLab key directly to PixelLab (https://pixellab.ai). PixelLab's own terms and privacy policy apply to those requests.

Cloud Save (optional)

If you connect Google Drive or Dropbox, PixelDoll copies your characters and their artwork to a folder in your own account so you can restore them and use them on your other devices: a "PixelDoll" folder in Google Drive, or "Apps/PixelDoll" in Dropbox. The files are ordinary compressed JSON and ZIP files of PNG images that you can open without PixelDoll. Your browser sends them directly to Google or Dropbox.

  • Google Drive access: PixelDoll asks only for permission to see, edit, create and delete the Drive files it creates itself (the drive.file scope), plus your email address, which is shown next to the connection. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Dropbox access: PixelDoll can read and write only its own app folder and read your account's email address.
  • Sign-in tokens: the sign-in is kept in this browser's storage so you stay connected. It is never shared with other devices or sent anywhere except to the provider.
  • Google sign-in helper: Google requires a server to finish and renew a sign-in. PixelDoll uses a small stateless function on Cloudflare Workers that passes your sign-in code or token to Google and returns Google's answer. It stores nothing and logs nothing, and never sees your files. This follows the token-mediating backend pattern of RFC 10017 (OAuth 2.0 for Browser-Based Applications).

PixelDoll's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google or Dropbox is used only to save and sync your PixelDoll library, and is never transferred to anyone else or used for any other purpose.

Stopping and deleting

  • Disconnect this device removes the sign-in from this browser. The cloud copy stays.
  • Disconnect everywhere (Google) withdraws PixelDoll's access for all your devices. For Dropbox, remove PixelDoll under your account's connected apps.
  • Delete cloud copy permanently deletes the PixelDoll folder in your cloud storage. Work saved on your devices stays.
  • You can also remove access at any time in your Google Account or Dropbox settings, and delete the folder yourself.
