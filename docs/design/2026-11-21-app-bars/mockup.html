Rules at a glance

Way out X icon, top-right, on every sheet, popover panel and toast. Alerts use Cancel instead.
Back Top-left, only in an inner pane. Never closes.
Footer Only for a commit. Note, then secondary, then primary last. Too narrow: stacks in the same order.
Retired labels Hide, Later, Not now, Done, Close and Cancel as sheet dismiss buttons.
Losing input X, Escape, backdrop and browser Back ask "Discard changes?" first.
Running work X closes and the job keeps running in Activity; Stop lives on the job.
Top bar Full screen and More last everywhere; Undo, Redo and Activity hide in reserved slots.
