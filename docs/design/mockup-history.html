285 existing mockup and palette-study images from October 20 through October 23, 2026, in oldest-to-newest order. Groups follow the documented design rounds; images within each group follow the local modification times recorded when this history was assembled. These timestamps help order the files but do not establish their original generation times. Wardrobe checks fall between the round-three drafts and final designs. Media availability: This gallery links to existing files using repository-relative paths. It contains no copied or embedded image data. The PNGs remain local and ignored by Git, so thumbnails require a checkout containing that media. The original 128 screenshots are also available in the historical archive at commit 1f95029. Later local images are not published by this index and may be missing in a fresh checkout or on GitHub.
