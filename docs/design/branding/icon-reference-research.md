# Icon reference search for PixelDoll

Research for a third logo round. These are references to study, not assets copied into PixelDoll. The search covered paper dolls, dress-up, clothes, wardrobe, dolls, paper craft, cutouts, pixel art, characters, sprite frames, layers, alignment, and fitting. Iconify keyword search returned many shirts and hangers but no direct results for the exact phrases `paper doll` or `dress up`; [Noun Project's paper-doll collection](https://thenounproject.com/browse/icons/term/paper-doll/) has a small set. This is a search result, not proof that no such icon exists.

## Literal paper-doll craft

| Reference | What to study | Logo risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Library of Congress paper doll and outfits](https://www.loc.gov/item/2006678949/) | Separate outfits with fold-over tabs: a concrete visual cue distinct from ordinary clothing icons | A whole doll-and-wardrobe composition will not survive a favicon |
| [Openclipart classic paper doll](https://openclipart.org/detail/237005/classic-paper-dolls) | How a garment's tabs advertise that it is removable | Avoid borrowing its illustration or vintage style |
| [Noun Project paper-doll icons](https://thenounproject.com/browse/icons/term/paper-doll/) | Existing visual shortcuts for a cutout figure | Most read as toys or chains of people rather than frame-aligned game equipment |

| [Tabler fold](https://tabler.io/icons/icon/fold) | An explicit fold in simple geometry | Can imply a document editor |

## What the search changes

The earlier marks tried to compress a character and garment into one tiny silhouette. The distinctive physical idea is **the removable garment tab**; the distinctive digital idea is **one fitted layer across fixed animation frames**. The third round should test these as bold shapes or negative space, including a custom P or D, before adding a small figure. A stock icon would be useful as a control in the comparison, not as the final logo.

Use the [Colorfetti master/export system](https://github.com/Shilo/colorfetti/blob/main/docs/research/2026-10-13-colorfetti-identity.md) as the production structure after a mark is chosen: transparent contrast variants for browser tabs, fixed opaque installed tiles, separately inset maskable icons, and one-color versions. Do not adopt Colorfetti's C silhouette or its palette.
