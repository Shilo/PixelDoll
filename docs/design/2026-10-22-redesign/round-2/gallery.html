PixelDoll / design round two

A little world.
Your character at its centre.

Two scenic pixel worlds. Four quieter prototype stages. Every direction includes desktop and phone character selection, creation and customization, plus a future walking-map study inspired by Asemulator.

Lead designer’s recommendation

Calibration Yard for creating. Moonwell Grove for arriving.

Use a restrained tile map for everyday work, and offer the grove as an optional collection backdrop. Keep native-size proofing one action away.
