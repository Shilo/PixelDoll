Bronze · Glass material comparison

Historical study. The current playable prototype uses @shilo/bronze-ui. These screenshots preserve the earlier GlassKit comparison. Reproduce its live material variants from the earlier Git revision; current material URL parameters are superseded. Open the current dependency comparison.

Recommendation: restrained glass. Keep the world, pixel artwork, bronze colors and open navigation. Use modest glass edges for playback and temporary surfaces. The rounded alternative demonstrates larger corners and a grouped view selector; it adds visual weight without improving character inspection.

Every comparison uses the same viewport and device scale. Screenshots are genuine browser captures; click an image to inspect it at full resolution. Before images were captured before integration.
