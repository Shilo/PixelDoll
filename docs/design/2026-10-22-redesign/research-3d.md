# RPG character creation and selection references

Research date and access date: **2026-10-22**. Scope: seven popular RPG, MMORPG, and life-simulation character interfaces, examined for patterns that might help PixelDoll's character and outfit workflow. This is evidence for the redesign, not a proposed screen or mockup.

## Evidence standard

- **Observed** means I inspected an image of the actual game interface embedded in the publisher's official page. It does not mean I used the game. I inspected interface images for **World of Warcraft: Warbands**, **Final Fantasy XIV**, and **Black Desert**. Image detail is limited by the size of the published screenshots.
- **Documented** means a publisher, developer, official manual, or clearly identified game wiki describes the behavior in text. A documented feature is not evidence of its exact current visual layout.
- **Inference** means a design implication for PixelDoll. Those claims are explicitly separate from source facts. The Guild Wars 2 Wiki is community authored, though dedicated to the game; I pair it with ArenaNet's own fashion article.

## Strongest transferable patterns

1. **One clear subject with a scalable roster.** WoW's official screenshot makes the staged characters memorable while the searchable list handles the full collection. Guild Wars 2's documented selection screen similarly distinguishes one selected model from other slots. For PixelDoll, a stage should identify the active character immediately, but the library needs an explicit count, searchable access as it grows, and selection cues that do not rely on the figure alone. This is an inference from the [WoW](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24061008/the-war-within-warbands-preview) and [GW2](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Character_selection_screen) evidence.
2. **A large, stable preview with task-specific controls.** FFXIV and Black Desert put the character in the visual center while panels change the attribute being edited. The Sims guide describes detailed editing from the character itself. PixelDoll can carry over subject focus, but it needs crisp integer scaling, a neutral inspection state, and controls for frame/direction/layer validation. These are inferences from the [FFXIV manual](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/game_manual/start/), [Black Desert guide](https://blackdesert.pearlabyss.com/Asia/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=9), and [EA guide](https://cdn-assets-ts4.pulse.ea.com/Guide/TheSims4_PlayersGuide_ENGLISH.pdf).
3. **Coarse choices first, refinement next.** EA's Styled Looks, BG3's named/custom starting routes, and GW2's slot/channel focus each offer a way to begin without exposing every control at once. For PixelDoll, this supports a quick outfit or category entry followed by specific item, recolor, pose, and export operations. This does **not** imply a mandatory wizard for every visit. [EA guide](https://cdn-assets-ts4.pulse.ea.com/Guide/TheSims4_PlayersGuide_ENGLISH.pdf); [Larian update](https://baldursgate3.game/news/community-update-21-forging-your-legacy_77); [ArenaNet fashion article](https://www.guildwars2.com/en/news/coming-soon-fashion-templates/).
4. **Inspect before committing.** FFXIV's gear/background/pose previews, Black Desert's clean UI toggle and edit history, and GW2's fashion preview/edit/save distinction all support judging the character in context before a consequential action. PixelDoll should expose actual available frame/direction coverage and item consistency before generation charges or export. [FFXIV manual](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/game_manual/start/); [Black Desert guide](https://blackdesert.pearlabyss.com/Asia/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=9); [ArenaNet fashion article](https://www.guildwars2.com/en/news/coming-soon-fashion-templates/).

## Decisions and cautions for the redesign

- **World stage:** The WoW camp image proves a cast can occupy a coherent place without turning selection into a navigable world. For PixelDoll, a quiet environment may add identity, but visual contrast must keep 48–56 px bodies, transparent layers, and stray pixels inspectable. A detailed scene is unsuitable as the only proofing background. This is an inference, not a documented game rule.
- **Cast versus library:** WoW's four staged favorites are explicitly a subset of a full Warband. If PixelDoll stages three or four sprites, show that the library can hold more and give direct list/grid access. The full library should remain discoverable on small screens. [Blizzard preview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24061008/the-war-within-warbands-preview).
- **Category rails:** FFXIV and Black Desert demonstrate meaningful grouping; their long and icon-heavy menus also reveal a density risk. A compact PixelDoll rail needs visible text for unfamiliar categories such as “Moves,” “Layers,” “Recolor,” and “Export,” or a nearby label that stays present without hover. This is a translation judgment based on their official screenshots and [W3C's guidance that visible labels should match accessible names](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/label-in-name).
- **Camera and orientation:** FFXIV's free camera and WoW's rotatable character cannot transfer literally. PixelDoll has authored directions and frames; a direction control must snap to available sprite directions, state when a view is missing, and disclose any mirrored preview. A drag gesture alone would suggest continuous 3D orbit and could hide missing layers. [FFXIV manual](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/game_manual/start/); [Blizzard patch notes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24119590/the-war-within-pre-expansion-content-update-notes-now-live).
- **Onboarding and costly actions:** FFXIV calls out progress loss and hard-to-change Home World at the relevant step; DD2 warns about finalized attributes. PixelDoll's honest friction is paid generation, destructive deletion, and potentially replacing a frozen base. Show cost and consequence at the action, preserve drafts where possible, and avoid ritual confirmation for reversible dressing. [FFXIV manual](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/game_manual/start/); [Capcom FAQ](https://www.capcom-support.com/hc/en-us/articles/13470568073756-Dragon-s-Dogma-2-Frequently-Asked-Questions-PlayStation-5).
- **Mobile and accessibility:** These references are mainly desktop/console interfaces, so their hover details, tiny edge controls, and broad horizontal stages cannot be copied to a narrow browser. PixelDoll's mobile translation should keep character identity and the active task visible, make roster and categories operable without hover, preserve keyboard focus, and give touch controls room. WCAG 2.2 AA sets a 24-by-24 CSS px pointer-target minimum with exceptions; [Apple recommends 44-by-44 pt controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/tips/) in its platform guidance. [W3C target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum); [W3C focus-visible guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-visible). These are standards/guidelines, not observations about the games.

## Rejections

- **Free orbit, face sculpting, and depth controls:** physically unsupported by a 2D, frame-based 64 px paperdoll. Available directions, poses, and frames are discrete assets.
- **Dense RPG menus and unlabeled icon clusters:** FFXIV and Black Desert have depth appropriate to their engines, but PixelDoll has fewer meaningful axes. Copying their density would obscure the sprite and create icon mystery on touch devices.
- **Fantasy chrome as the interface system:** WoW/Black Desert environmental art can be a reference for mood. Ornate frames, faux materials, and glowing controls would compete with pixel inspection and conflict with PixelDoll's stated simple, custom-CSS visual direction.
- **World navigation to reach editing:** no reference establishes that traversing a scene improves character creation. It adds steps between roster, dressing, quality checks, and export.
- **Game-specific finality and economy:** DD2's appearance item, GW2's transmutation charges, and Black Desert's coupon are mechanics of those products. PixelDoll's actual paid API calls need explicit cost clarity, without invented currencies or locks.

## Implications for shortlisted directions (research assessment only)

| Direction under consideration | Source support | Evidence-based concern to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Camp: three-character in-world roster** | WoW directly supports a small staged cast alongside a full list. | The three figures must be clearly a featured subset; the full library needs count, browse/search, and a route to every saved character. Small sprites may disappear against scenery, and a camp must not add travel/navigation. |
| **Atelier: one hero plus portrait rail and wardrobe tray** | GW2's selected model plus other slots, FFXIV's central preview and categories, and Sims Styled Looks support focused editing. | Portraits alone may not reveal item differences at 64 px; test whether the main sprite remains large enough when the tray opens on portrait phones. |
| **Quiet Stage: minimal stage plus compact list** | FFXIV and Black Desert show the value of a subject-centered inspection space; WoW supports a list separate from the scene. | Can look anonymous unless actual characters and names give it identity. A compact list must still expose creation, saved count, and selected state without tiny targets. |

## Open uncertainties

1. How many saved characters do typical PixelDoll users keep, and how often do they switch between them versus edit one? This determines whether a cast, portrait rail, or library list should dominate.
2. At what displayed scale do 48/56 px figures and 96 px weapon layers remain legible without compromising pixel-perfect inspection? Test on both desktop and narrow phones.
3. Which direction and clip combinations exist for an in-progress character? The interface should not imply complete orbit or animation coverage when frames have not been generated or have failed quality gates.
4. Which appearance operations are truly reversible after an item generation or base freeze, and what can be previewed offline before another paid call? Confirm against implementation before writing irreversible-action copy.
5. How should the stage background affect visual quality checks? Validate on a neutral backdrop and any atmospheric backdrop for alignment, transparency, and contrast.
