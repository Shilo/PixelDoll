## Intended outcome

PixelDoll should feel deliberately designed for dressing and evaluating pixel characters, with a coherent relationship between artwork, controls and work surfaces. Give simplicity-first usability and immersion priority: direct frequent actions, strong defaults, minimal setup, readable content, clear state, reliable recovery and visible paid consequences.

The concern is cumulative visual sameness, not a demonstrable AI-authorship signature. The current combination of matte charcoal surfaces, restrained sans-serif labels, thin line icons, rounded outlined controls and tinted selections can feel like a generic tool. A custom theme alone does not resolve that impression. The coworker's feedback specifically identified palette, matte flatness, typography and a lack of intentional UI art; it is one viewer's qualitative feedback, not a population study.

Preserve the strengths already observed: a prominent actual character, open stage rather than dashboard containment, contextual equipment with real item artwork, spatial facing/mirror controls, the size guide, exact palette work, readable source pixels, clear export grouping, cost disclosures and a complete light appearance. Familiar buttons, system typography and rounded corners may remain the best solution. Do not replace them merely to look unusual.

## Recommended experiment, not a predetermined answer

**Tactile Bronze is the recommended first candidate:** distinct recessed field backing, shallow primary/secondary action treatment, one restrained work-surface rim, and selected-state persistence during hover. **Bronze inlay** adds the optional selection edge. The [proposal table](../design/2026-11-08-bronze-materials/README.md#recommendation-and-purpose) explains each purpose, risk and proposed disposition.

Keep the baseline eligible to win. First hold fonts, geometry, content, source artwork and base palette constant to isolate the material hypothesis. Test a later typography or world-art alternative separately if the concern remains. Do not introduce arbitrary theme modes, a new UI kit, ornamental fantasy chrome, pixel fonts everywhere or extra setup to signal originality. Decoration can be justified, but novelty alone does not justify it.

For each substantial accepted change, record the observed problem, user purpose, chosen treatment, strongest alternative including no change, tradeoff and evidence/confidence in the existing review or plan. Keep small corrections proportionate. Do not use an AI-detector score or claim that aesthetic preference proves authorship.

## Acceptance checklist

- Simplicity-first workflows, character prominence, source pixels, palette editing, cost/consent boundaries, validation and recovery remain intact. No unrequested feature loss or extra setup.
- The chosen design improves a stated hierarchy, affordance, readability, state-clarity or coherent-identity concern in real application context. A new accent, font, texture or radius alone is not success.
- Primary, secondary, selected, hovered, pressed, keyboard-focused, disabled/busy and invalid states remain distinguishable as applicable. Selection remains clear while another element has focus. Test joined seams, swatches and scrollports for clipped indicators.
- Check Characters, New character, Preview, live views, Equipment, representative help/popovers, Import and Export in dark/light. Include actual long content, empty/search states, failures/recovery and relevant cost displays; do not send paid requests to manufacture these states.
- Review desktop, phone portrait, shallow landscape and 320 px reflow/zoom as applicable. Confirm source-art visibility, readable control text, non-overlapping targets and reachable dialog/footer/scroll content.
- Verify keyboard and native overlay behavior directly; evaluate meaningful contrast against actual backings, including preference fallbacks. Label assistive-technology, physical-device or platform combinations that remain untested.
- In Bronze, follow its current package, build, browser and contract gates. In PixelDoll, follow AGENTS.md: `npm run check`, `npm test`, appropriate end-to-end specs, and the full local suite when the change meets its cross-cutting criteria. Follow experience-screenshot configuration rules; do not invent a shorter test exception for a shared-theme release.
- Where feasible, ask unfamiliar users to identify the current choice, next action and paid consequence, and explain which comparison feels coherent. Record misses and comments without labeling one version AI-made. Small formative observations do not establish a universal preference.
