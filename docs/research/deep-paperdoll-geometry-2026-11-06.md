# Equipment geometry, visibility, and motion evidence

November 6, 2026. Read-only source audit and independent offline probes against PixelDoll's actual TypeScript functions. No PixelLab requests or third-party installs were made. The external code was read in pinned snapshots or, where indicated, as a public browser bundle. The proposals below need their own qualification before changing user artwork.

## What existing paper dolls make explicit

| Terraria via tModLoader | The draw schedule has distinct back hair, back head, back accessories, torso, held item, and arm-over-item stages. | Stardew Valley's Fashion Sense mod | Each appearance can override draw order and hide sleeves, the body, or a waterline conditionally. Color masks identify explicit source colors and include premultiplied-alpha handling. DrawPatch consumes equipped models and a single farmer animation frame, then draws sorted layers. [Appearance model](https://github.com/Floogen/FashionSense/blob/39e9e9e13c9f9a78416172e73a229cf8703ddb59/FashionSense/Framework/Models/Appearances/AppearanceModel.cs) · [Renderer](https://github.com/Floogen/FashionSense/blob/39e9e9e13c9f9a78416172e73a229cf8703ddb59/FashionSense/Framework/Patches/Renderer/DrawPatch.cs#L299) | Item-specific depth, compatibility, and dye ownership are separate. | Exult and Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup | Exult chooses paperdoll arm art based on the arm/weapon state and uses shape coverage for the interaction. Crawl authors wearable/wieldable versions separately from inventory icons, with a base-type fallback for missing artifacts and body-form-specific variants. [Exult paper doll](https://github.com/exult/exult/blob/dba00df48237c4b33862f0a9892a8001f75d6eb8/gumps/Paperdoll_gump.cc#L684) · [Crawl art contract](https://github.com/crawl/crawl/blob/b9b84643cfc412639d6eb8342955b6f9508d88bd/crawl-ref/docs/develop/tiles_creation.txt) · [Crawl doll resolution](https://github.com/crawl/crawl/blob/b9b84643cfc412639d6eb8342955b6f9508d88bd/crawl-ref/source/tiledoll.cc) | An equipment design, an inventory icon, and fitted sprites are distinct assets. A grip-compatible bow stance or two-handed move may require a different frozen body move. Treat that as a deliberate compatibility contract. Keep per-asset art notices when building an external fixture set. |
| Spine and Godot | Spine decouples attachment slots from bones, keys the order, and confines clipping to a draw range. Godot's 2D deformation guidance combines weighted meshes and discrete overlapping cutouts. [Slots](https://en.esotericsoftware.com/spine-slots) · [Draw-order keys](https://us.esotericsoftware.com/spine-keys) · [Clipping](https://en.esotericsoftware.com/spine-clipping) · [Godot deformation](https://godotengine.org/article/godot-gets-2d-skeletal-deform/) | An attachment's transform and visibility require different inputs. A hard permission mask is useful when scoped to a specific asset and layer. |
| DTWorldz Sprite Capture Studio | The published workflow uses catalog Blender packages, independent left/right captures and a shared camera and fixed origin. [Product documentation](https://spritecapturestudio.dtworldz.com/docs) · [Developer write-up](https://dtworldz.com/blog/introducing-dtworldz-sprite-capture-studio) · [Inspected browser module](https://spritecapturestudio.dtworldz.com/_next/static/chunks/capture-studio-Dhm2ZdWH.js) | Use a clean 3D orthographic capture as a depth and ownership oracle for tests. Shipping a guessed full depth map would increase the export contract without resolving that ambiguity. |
| EasyRPG Player and libGDX | EasyRPG draws fixed source cells with center-bottom origins. libGDX supports whitespace stripping while retaining original size and offsets and restores the sprite origin during drawing. Atlas trimming is a serialization optimization that needs original rect offsets, compatible sampling and parity tests. It does not align the garment to the body. |

## Research that separates geometry from newly visible pixels

- Moving least squares supports rigid, similarity, and affine 2D transformations. Local control points reduce global distortion. An inverse map from output pixel centers avoids the holes of a forward splat, though quantization can still break one-pixel features. A mesh or warp cannot infer a new view of a surface. Aseprite exposes RotSprite alongside its faster rotation precisely because pixel topology matters. Benchmark an inverse integer-sampled rigid transform and compare any edge-aware variant on one-pixel straps and sword tips before bringing either into production. [Schaefer, McPhail and Warren](https://www.cs.rice.edu/~jwarren/research/mls.pdf) · [Aseprite rotation](https://aseprite.org/docs/rotate/) · [RotSprite implementation](https://github.com/aseprite/aseprite/blob/main/src/doc/algorithm/rotsprite.cpp)
- First Order Motion Model predicts both a dense warp and an occlusion map. Thin-Plate Spline Motion Model improves the deformation field and uses multi-resolution occlusion, with a pixel-art horse among its qualitative examples. Deformable Sprites represents a layer with a persistent texture, per-frame visibility, and a deformation field. These papers support separating stable design from pose-specific visibility and asking a generator only for uncertain visible pixels. They provide no accuracy claims for 32–160 px humanoid gear or PixelLab's conditioning. [FOMM project](https://aliaksandrsiarohin.github.io/first-order-model-website/) · [TPS paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Zhao_Thin-Plate_Spline_Motion_Model_for_Image_Animation_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf) · [Deformable Sprites](https://deformable-sprites.github.io/)
- TAPIR and CoTracker predict position and visibility across frames. The transferable idea is to make a track abstain when a point disappears or reappears. PixelDoll already stores requested and measured pose tracks and implements bounded image patch tracking. Test forward/backward agreement, match distinctiveness and measured/requested joint disagreement before a track authorizes a hard mask. Uncertain anchors still help a review overlay. [TAPIR](https://deepmind-tapir.github.io/) · [CoTracker](https://github.com/facebookresearch/co-tracker) · [PixelDoll tracking](../../app/src/lib/rig/skeleton.ts)
- Omnimatte separates an object from its environment and can associate effects with the object. Generative Omnimatte also attempts to reconstruct fully visible layers. Cryptomatte gets identity from a scene during rendering. These techniques extract visible layers, infer hidden art, or record exact renderer ownership, respectively. PixelDoll can use exact ownership for synthetic evaluation and maintain uncertainty for flattened output. A hallucinated hidden pixel must never become a silently approved new costume detail. [Omnimatte](https://omnimatte.github.io/) · [Generative Omnimatte](https://gen-omnimatte.github.io/) · [Cryptomatte specification](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Psyop/Cryptomatte/master/specification/cryptomatte_specification.pdf)

The deeper work builds on all four November 6 spikes. The [motion study](tool-spikes-2026-11-06-motion.md) found tracked translation can lose to fixed alignment on helmets and fail on capes. The [layering study](tool-spikes-2026-11-06-layering.md) showed why a whole-body alpha stencil destroys large fractions of cape and sword pixels, and why editing a slot-wide override from an item inspector surprises users. The [generation study](tool-spikes-2026-11-06-generation.md) found neighboring masks can help a partially clipped result but cannot guarantee a missing design, and that relative seam scores can amplify tiny changes. The [chroma study](tool-spikes-2026-11-06-chroma-and-occlusion.md) showed a foreground classification can hide body repaint from key-retention QA. Earlier [rig research](2026-10-27-animation-rigging-sources.md) covers retargeted motion, root constraints, joint depths and why 2D projection can become singular.

## Experiments in order of risk

1. **Make quality decisions independent of batch composition.** Correct timeline adjacency and clip-local temporal support with the above fixtures, then replay retained shield masks, standing accessories, long hoods and incomplete repair outputs. Verify previously detected quarter turns still fail. Compare saved accepted artwork before and after separately from any changed warning. This needs zero provider calls.
2. **Create a small independent visibility benchmark.** Author arrays or render a synthetic proxy whose body, near hand, far hand, helmet, front hair, back hair, cape, shield and weapon each have an explicit owner. Include a hand over a shaft with the blade over the torso, hair crossing a shoulder, cape over far arm but behind near arm, a circlet with an intentional opening, mirrored and genuinely asymmetric views, sitting and prone, and missing-vs-authorized-transparent cells. Export from one project frame clock and compare each engine's render against the labeled composite at a shared pivot. Track wrong-owner pixels at true overlaps, protected base preservation and mask recall independently. Require zero dropped valid pixels in hard-mask controls, including one-pixel details. For import/preview tests retain full cells and verify native-nearest render before evaluating atlases.
3. **Use a rigid projection as evidence and a potential no-cost repair.** Fit a 2D translation plus bounded rotation from the approved design and the intended head/wrist anchor, use inverse nearest sampling from the original design in every cell and retain exact alpha/palette. Forearm length or head-axis ambiguity is a rejection signal. Compare the baseline translation, rotation, and current PixelLab results on the same body, pose, direction and approved design. Include poses where a 2D transform cannot account for yaw, hand articulation, or cape billow. On masks with known alpha compare recall, false ownership and border loss. On saved provider output use blinded acceptance instead of treating output similarity as truth. Where a wrist/hand occluder is known, restore only the frozen hand pixels in the bounded grip region. Initially emit a review candidate and separate provenance.
4. **Spend generations where the candidate is uncertain.** Measure a broad masked edit against a hybrid proposal. From the same approved direction and frozen clip, form an editable union of projected support, confirmed matching neighboring designs and bounded slot allowance. Restore frozen protected pixels after the response. Keep the current broad request available when the mask lacks coverage or anchor confidence is low. Measure actual accepted frames per billed generation, independent false-removal/extra-pixel counts, and time to accepted review. Hold the same approved design, base pixels, region protocol, prompt family and seeded request setup constant across arms, repeat across multiple seeds and retain failed samples. Mark which predictions are permitted to expand from a new viewpoint.
5. **Give artists a narrow escape hatch for semantic depth.** Test per-item role assignment on a single flagged frame or series first. Reuse the existing `fg` and `bg` layer representation where sufficient, and treat a drawn occluder or clipping mask as data independent from alpha. Two overlapping gear items should keep their intended ordering when a third item is added. Check interaction with hair tuck, base cuts, recolor, mirroring and runtime depth ranking. A single baked pair per cover cannot express arbitrary simultaneous cuts, and a generalized pairwise variant library scales with combinations. Verify the actual use cases before adding any runtime depth format.

Keep the PixelLab design source and its approved directional views immutable through local experiments. During review, separate provider artwork, pixels protected by the frozen pose, and the editable region assigned to the equipment. Promote deterministic changes when the same evidence explains both the improvement and the cases where the pipeline abstains.
