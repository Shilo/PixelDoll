create-character-state · 10 gens · about 2 min · once per item

Create a state with the item

PixelLab redraws the character's standing views in all 8 directions at once, now wearing the helmet. The result is a new PixelLab character, and it only has standing views, no animations.

+→
5
animate the new character · per move, per direction, for every item

Animate the dressed character

Because the state is a separate character, every move has to be generated again for it. PixelLab draws a whole new walk: the helmet moves, but so does every pixel of the body, in its own way.

Our code · cut out

Cut out the item

Taking the changed pixels gives the helmet plus pieces of the redrawn body. With a green body (step 3) the green would come off cleanly, but the helmet would still be drawn around the new body from step 5, not yours.

8
Our code · recompose()

Put it on your real body

The cut-out over your walk from step 2. It looks right only because it carries the other walk's body pieces along; a clean helmet layer would not sit on your body's head the same way.

The useful part of this idea: step 4 is a strong dresser. It dresses all 8 directions in one call with one consistent design, has no 80 px frame limit, and stays clean on a green body. If its dressed standing view goes into today's step 5 (posed with your joints) instead of being animated on its own, the item keeps fitting your body. That combination is the plan for characters larger than 80 px (board card "Dress characters larger than 80 px").

Pros and cons

Today: dress once, pose with the same jointsYour idea 1: create stateYour idea 2: edit image
Fits your bodyYes: 0–6 % of body pixels offNo, when the dressed character is animated on its ownNo: about half the body pixels off
Paid calls for one item (idle + walk, 3 directions)4 calls, 19 gens1 state call (10 gens) + a 0.25 import + 6 animations of the new character, again for every item3 sheets at 25 gens (36 frames + references), 75 gens
Pros
  • Item lines up with the body in every frame
  • One design in every frame
  • Cheapest per item
  • All 8 directions dressed in one call, including real west views (no mirrored weapon hand)
  • Most consistent design between directions
  • No 80 px frame limit; clean on a green body
  • One call covers many frames
  • Pictures up to 512 px
Cons
  • Needs the skeleton, so the item moves only as much as the joints do (stiff idle, rigid hair)
  • PixelLab draws the body twice (bare and dressed)
  • Above 80 px the dress call fits only 4 frames
  • Only dresses standing views, never animation frames
  • The dressed character is a separate character: every move must be generated again per item, and each frame is a new body
  • Needs a green re-import to cut out cleanly
  • Moves and redraws the bodies, so items do not fit
  • Loses the green on many cells
  • Only 1 picture per call above 128 px
VerdictKeep (in the app)Keep the dress step (step 4) for large characters, combined with today's posing; drop animating the dressed character on its ownRejected
