**Verified public contract.** The [Poses API](https://www.autosprite.io/docs/api-poses) represents a pose as an image that can be generated, uploaded, or saved from a sheet and reused as a first or last frame. The [regular export](https://www.autosprite.io/docs/reference-export-formats) is a transparent PNG grid and frame/timing JSON. The documented contract has no equipment-layer, palette-ramp, or shared-pivot equipment map. The walkthrough and export evidence support complete dressed animation output. They do not establish whether the service internally decomposes the asset or offers an undocumented layer export.

**Reported costs and speed.** The [credits reference](https://www.autosprite.io/docs/reference-credits) lists 5 credits for a 2-second Turbo clip, 10/15 for 4/6-second Pro, 10 per second for Ultra, and 35/53 for 4/6-second Max. The [FAQ](https://www.autosprite.io/docs/faq) reports 30–60 seconds per animation and 3–5 minutes for five clips, plus a $5 pack for 100 credits. At that pack rate, regenerating one Turbo clip for another look represents $0.25 in clip credits before pose or wardrobe costs. Wardrobe batch prices and repeatable wall times were not disclosed in the reviewed material. The credit guide lists a 3-credit generated pose while the Poses API lists 5 credits for its generation call, so those surfaces should not be treated as one price. Vendor timings and the live demo are different measurements from PixelDoll's recorded elapsed time. No sign-in, purchase, or service generation was performed.

**Challenge to PixelDoll.** The useful UX is a labeled design source and pose target, with a reusable intermediate and a visible approval boundary. PixelDoll's [New Item sheet](../../app/src/studio/NewItemSheet.svelte#L72) and [item details](../../app/src/studio/ItemPane.svelte#L185) already stage standing design and subsequent coverage. [Shared Design fitting](../../app/src/studio/FitDesignSheet.svelte#L152) goes further with separately priced standing and move stages. The gap is that a player reviewing a difficult result may still need to infer whether the defect came from the standing design, a particular posed direction, or extraction. Prototype a side-by-side accepted standing reference and failed current frame inside the existing repair review before considering a separate Mix Bench. Keep one approved design as the default. Preserve the existing exact recoloring and per-slot export instead of regenerating every outfit clip.

## Slavikson Consistency Characters v0.4

**Verified metadata.** The [Civitai listing](https://civitai.com/models/2047895/sonsistency-characters-or-generate-characters-only-by-image-and-prompt-without-characters-lora-or-ilnoobai-edit) and [public metadata API](https://civitai.com/api/v1/models/2047895) identify author Slavikson, Illustrious base family, and versions 0.1 through 0.4. The creator describes a still-image reference plus character prompt, recommends matching the source checkpoint when possible, and explicitly reports sensitivity to seed. Version 0.4 (ID 2363451) adds OpenPose and Depth ControlNet support, removes an indirect routing mechanism to clarify node relationships, and lists fixes. It ships an approximately 8.26 MiB archive. Direct file download returned HTTP 401 with authentication required. The same status on the public green and red mirrors ruled out a transient metadata access problem. Example metadata did not contain a graph. The archive, exact node wiring, strengths, sampler, checkpoint compatibility, layer output, and repeatability remain unverified. [Versioned archive endpoint](https://civitai.com/api/download/models/2363451?fileId=2254216)

**Mechanism and limits.** The [ControlNet authors](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Zhang_Adding_Conditional_Control_to_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_ICCV_2023_paper.html) show that spatial conditions such as depth and human pose can guide diffusion. A skeleton describes joint placement, while depth can distinguish visible overlap and relative placement that a projected joint track leaves ambiguous. For paper dolling this distinction matters at arms crossing a garment, hands holding weapons, capes behind limbs, and same-pose foreshortening. It remains an inference that the specific Civitai graph resolves any of those cases on pixel art. Neither its still-image listing nor its author notes establish frozen pixels, frame-aligned layers, batch coverage, or a reusable video loop. Seed dependence argues against trusting one favorable still as qualification.

**Challenge to PixelDoll.** PixelDoll already separates a standing style reference and a moving geometry reference in [pose submission](../../app/src/lib/items/run.ts#L200). [Shared Design transfer](../../app/src/lib/equipment/generate.ts#L89) already carries design pixels separately from the target frozen standing frames. The gap is that joints alone leave visible garment depth and occlusion ambiguous, and prompting cannot certify body preservation. Keep the reference distinction and test stronger pixel-level constraints using allowed PixelLab routes. Use actual frozen target frames as the geometry source when evaluating frame editing, one same-direction approved reference for design, and the existing extraction and acceptance gates for output. Start with occlusion cases where the overlap differs while a pose looks similar. Test reuse across at least two silhouettes and camera directions before promoting arbitrary concept-image fitting. Do not add Illustrious, a ControlNet backend, or unconstrained full-character regeneration to the production pipeline.

## ComfyUI 2D character pipeline and samples

**Verified static source at `9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c`.** Read the clone, workflow JSON, drivers, docs, and tracked sample PNGs. The full inference pipeline was not installed or run. [Repository](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/tree/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c)

- W1 creates a useful still pose by editing a source character. W2 starts from that one keyframe and uses Wan video generation. The W2 graph has no last-frame input or seam verifier. W3 semantically segments the base, resizes to 128 px, typically converts to luma, then stitches the first requested frames in a horizontal strip. The runtime picks loop behavior. [Pipeline overview](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/blob/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c/docs/pipeline-overview.md)
- W4 rough pass segments the single dressed still, expands its mask, broadcasts it across the base clip, and fills it with Wan VACE. The optional clean pass segments each rough dressed frame, grows that silhouette, and inpaints the **original** base frames, then explicitly composites in the mask. That compositing node preserves decoded base pixels where the mask is zero at that step. The MP4 transport, resize, and separately segmented soft layer cannot substantiate the broader promise of bit-exact final PNG composition. [W4 guide](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/blob/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c/docs/workflow-4-cosmetic-inpaint.md) · [Clean graph](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/blob/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c/workflows/w4-cosmetic-inpaint/pass2_clean_api.json)
- W4's current driver ships with pass 2 disabled and a 10 px offset if enabled, while the guide lists 3 px. Those values are at video resolution and cannot be copied as 1:1 PixelDoll pixel thresholds. The driver reports the optional pass as extra inference, and the guide estimates roughly 1.5–3 minutes in its setup. [Driver](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/blob/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c/drivers/run_w4_cosmetic_inpaint.py#L65) · [W4 guide](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/blob/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c/docs/workflow-4-cosmetic-inpaint.md)
- W5 is implemented even though W4's introduction still says otherwise. It segments the final cosmetic clip per frame, shapes the mask, builds alpha, and stitches cells in the same order as W3. A separate green-key iris pass supports a tintable sublayer. Samples verify 33 cells for idle and walking and nine for in-air, with an intentional faceless source. The README lists two hair sets and other eye styles that are absent from this checkout. Actual files include shirt, pants, face, eyes, and iris examples with uneven move coverage. [W5](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/blob/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c/docs/workflow-5-cosmetic-spritesheet.md) · [Sample directory](https://github.com/mor-o/comfyui-2d-character-pipeline/tree/9fee239216b8fb5a841401644a4ef629496e563c/samples)
- Sample inspection reproduces soft matte coverage. Among pixels with alpha above zero in the 33-frame walking strips, 50.1% of shirt pixels, 56.9% of pants pixels, 64.0% of eye-style-3 pixels, and 81.9% of face pixels have alpha below 255. The measured RGB-and-shape transition distance also reaches 0.63, 0.77, 0.56, and 0.93 within those four clips. Motion and occlusion contribute to this distance, so it cannot by itself diagnose flicker. Same cell coordinates and layout establish registration. Neither establishes stable cosmetic edges, palette identity, or body protection at composite time.

### Offline probe 1: how much can a tight repair mask recover?

Used the S2 helmet/cape east walks, x28 edit-animation cloak east walk and attack layers, and the x26 skeleton-posed east cloak attack after production `chromaLayer` extraction against the matching authored bare frames. Tested all eight cells per clip. Treat the stored opaque pixels as the target, then synthesize one failed cell at a time by deleting its rightmost four columns or its entire mask. Compare square dilation by 1 or 2 px against the failed mask alone and 2 px dilation of its union with the previous and next original mask. Use cyclic neighbors for the walks and open ends for attacks. The adjacent cells are intentionally treated as reliable in this single-failure stress test. Coverage is covered target pixels / all target pixels, summed over cells. Exposed pixels are mean mask area per cell minus covered target pixels. They are available for unwanted edits and are not measured model damage.

| Clip | Clipped edge covered, failed grow 1 / 2 | Clipped edge covered, adjacent union grow 2 | Missing item covered, adjacent union grow 2 | Mean exposed pixels, adjacent union on clipped edge |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| S2 cape walk east | 98.2% / 98.9% | 100% | 100% | 279 |
| S2 helmet walk east | 88.8% / 93.2% | 100% | 100% | 135 |
| x28 cloak walk east | 97.6% / 98.6% | 100% | 99.5% | 494 |
| x28 cloak attack east | 97.8% / 98.6% | 100% | 100% | 385 |
| x26 skeleton cloak attack east | 98.4% / 99.0% | 99.7% | 96.2% | 986 |

Failed-mask growth by itself covers **0%** of a missing item. It covers all known pixels when the silhouette is intact, so it is appropriate to qualify on a defect such as color bleed, an internal blemish, or a small extra piece with otherwise stable coverage. The adjacent-mask union exposes extra regions, especially for the x26 cloak with larger shape changes. It can still miss real item pixels, where production's exact mask enforcement would prevent recovery. The probe uses the app's `bandMask(bandOf(slot, undefined, height), width, height)` as its no-joints comparison, where 4,096 or 6,400 pixels are editable. Live rig repair uses measured joints and garment extent, so these figures do not estimate the gain against a live joint-aware band. Synthetic target pixels are evidence of geometric coverage only. No model quality gain, price decrease, or production false-positive rate was measured.

**Current PixelDoll behavior.** [gridReq](../../app/src/lib/items/run.ts#L148) already uses an approved same-direction dressed reference in a protected reference cell. The input cells start from the frozen chroma body, with a joint-aware vertical band expanded for the approved garment or a radius around the measured hand. The output is constrained by the exact submitted [stencil](../../app/src/lib/items/run.ts#L330), including in [single-clip repair](../../app/src/lib/items/run.ts#L663). [Prior clean-grip evidence](2026-11-02-clean-grip-qualification.md) records a narrow mask that preserved the body but still lost or changed weapon parts. Using a thin failed mask for every repair would undo deliberate room for full silhouettes and remove the recovery region when an item vanishes.

### Offline probe 2: loop seam and false positives

Decode the stored PNGs. Slice sheets into cells without recentering. For ComfyUI use the same alpha threshold of 128 as the probe uses for all other inputs. For two frames, score the union of occupied pixels: 1 for a mismatch in occupancy, otherwise the largest absolute RGB channel difference divided by 255. Divide by occupied union size. Compare last-to-first with consecutive within-clip distances. For a candidate earlier ending, consider frames after the midpoint and minimize the same first-frame distance. Frame numbers below are one-based. This measures rendered transitions on a common pivot. It has no ground truth for gait phase, contact, timing, or perceived quality.

| Clip | Last → first | Median within | Max within | Earlier ending candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| S2 walk south / east / north | 0.382 / 0.479 / 0.387 | 0.183 / 0.495 / 0.211 | 0.373 / 0.550 / 0.310 | 5 / 5 / 5 |
| S2 cross-punch south, one-shot control | 0.694 | 0.459 | 0.632 | 4 |
| x28 PixMinimax walk south / east / north | 0.506 / 0.694 / 0.599 | 0.249 / 0.467 / 0.391 | 0.326 / 0.553 / 0.519 | 8 / 6 / 6 |
| x26 managed idle, skeleton render east | 0.212 | 0.130 | 0.166 | None |
| ComfyUI base idle | 0.026 | 0.010 | 0.076 | 32 |
| ComfyUI base walking | 0.278 | 0.189 | 0.269 | 31 |
| ComfyUI in-air, one-shot control | 0.263 | 0.187 | 0.411 | 7 |

The absolute visual distance between ComfyUI idle endpoints is 0.026, yet its relative score exceeds 2.5 times the median. Ratio alone is misleading. A rule that requires absolute size and a meaningful excess over normal transitions can flag a pronounced wrap without marking every idle and every deliberately one-shot attack. The x26 skeleton idle counterexample challenges the assumption that a cyclic request guarantees rendered continuity. The x28 candidates cut off different amounts of motion in different directions. The silhouette may match despite the limbs moving in the opposite phase. Treat these as review hints. Exact duplicated endpoints can also produce a hold if the timing includes both copies.

**Current PixelDoll behavior.** [Body clip QA](../../app/src/lib/rig/clips.ts#L97) checks adherence, palette, framing, bottom drift, connectivity, and facing. It has no dedicated rendered wrap diagnostic. [Equipment QA](../../app/src/lib/items/items.ts#L933) does compare wrap neighbors for shape and orientation, which is a different question and can miss a position or color pop. [Rig playback](../../app/src/lib/rig/rig.ts#L30) and [Export playback settings](../../app/src/lib/store/export-settings.ts#L103) separate looping metadata from pixel identities. Studio preview/share always loops by design. A diagnostic should use the intended motion semantics and actual cell playlist, account for held/repeated frames, and leave the shared body/equipment frame indices intact. Imported user-authored clips need separate calibration.
