# R1: PixelLab API contract and prices

Date: 2026-11-24. Read-only. Evidence tags: **V** = verified (code/contract/usage record), **D** = derived (arithmetic), **H** = hypothesis (needs a paid test). Every price in generations (gen); USD = gen x 0.005 at the published 10,000-generation plan rate (`plans.ts:17-19`, `docs/pixellab-plans.md:23-27`).

## Scope and method

Read: `docs/research/pixellab/pixellab-openapi-v2.json` (queried with node scripts per request/response schema, never dumped), `app/src/lib/pixellab/routes.ts` (all typed builders), `app/src/lib/pixellab/plans.ts`, `docs/pixellab-plans.md`, `docs/research/2026-11-23-item-move-route-cost.md` (desk table + cost ledger), `app/src/lib/items/items.ts` price bands, `app/src/lib/rig/clips.ts` skeleton price, and R5 `reports/R5-cost-model-and-test-design.md`. Not read: `queue.ts` (114 KB), the x31 spike ledger in full. No calls, no tests.

Usage-record basis used below is the 674 paid generations in the x31 ledger (`2026-11-23-item-move-route-cost.md:30`) plus the earlier live checks it cites (`docs/pixellab-plans.md:70,92`).

## 2. Exact answers

**Which routes price by frame count vs canvas (V).**

- Frame count only: `/animate-with-skeleton-v3` (2/3/4 gens for 3/8/15 frames, explicitly "not on canvas size").
- Size and frame count: `/animate-pixminimax` and `/characters/animations` mode=pixminimax; `/animate-with-text-v3` constrains size x frames by a 524,288 px budget but states no price formula (H: same shape as pixminimax).
- Canvas only, independent of frame count: `/edit-animation-v2` (longest side), `/edit-images-v2` and `/inpaint-v3` (mean side), `/create-character-v3` (area x 8 / 65536 per direction set), `/edit-image-pro-flash` (native size band).
- Flat: standard 8-direction create (1), `/edit-image-pixen` (1), prompt enhancement routes (0.05), full-direction import (0.25).
- Consequence (D): on canvas-priced routes, packing more frames or views into one canvas is free. At 92 px, 3 views x 6 frames on one 392x270 sheet costs 15 vs 60 anchored, and 4 views x 8 frames on 392x360 costs 25 (`2026-11-23-item-move-route-cost.md:38-40,100`). The lever is canvas area, not frame count.

**True max canvas per route (V unless noted).**
256x256: `create-character-v3` output, `create-character-pro` stored canvas, `create-character-pro-flash`, `/characters/animations` in all modes, `edit-animation-v2` output (16-256, per-frame input 1-256), `transfer-outfit-v2`, `edit-image-pixen`, `edit-image-pro-flash`, `animate-with-skeleton-v3`, `/animate-with-text-v3`, `/animate-pixminimax`, `create-character-state` (multiples of 4).
512x512: `edit-images-v2` (output and each input), `inpaint-v3` (inpainting image and mask).
1024: `create-character-pro` concept image only; `inpaint-v3` context image (deprecated, no longer in the typed client).
128: `create-character-with-8-directions` requested `image_size` (16-128); standard mode may return a canvas larger than the requested `image_size`; the contract does not state by how much, and we have no usage record (unverified).
So the largest usable _item or body_ frame side today is 256, and `/edit-images-v2` is the only route that keeps a >256 canvas in play.

**Routes that accept multiple images and the cap (V).**

- `/edit-images-v2`: `edit_images` maxItems **16**, minItems 1 (contract), effectively 16/9/4/1 by size and one fewer with `reference_image`.
- `/edit-animation-v2`: `frames` maxItems **16**, minItems **2** (`EDIT_MIN_FRAMES`, `routes.ts:38`), effectively 16/9/4 by longest side.
- `/transfer-outfit-v2`: `frames` maxItems **16**, minItems 2, effectively 15/8/3 plus one `reference_image`.
- `/create-character-v3`: `reference_image` + `direction_images` up to 7 (8 dirs) or 3 (4 dirs).
- `/create-character-with-8-directions`: `directions` map up to 8 + `color_image`; `create-character-pro`: concept + reference + `style_character_id`.
- `/inpaint-v3`: 1 image + 1 mask (+ deprecated context).
- Two images: `/animate-with-text-v3` and `/animate-pixminimax` (`first_frame` + `last_frame`).

**Routes that return many outputs (V).** One call returning several images: `/edit-images-v2` (<=16), `/edit-animation-v2` (<=16), `/transfer-outfit-v2` (<=15), `/animate-with-skeleton-v3` (3-15), `/animate-with-text-v3` (4-16), `/animate-pixminimax` (4-40), every `create-character*` (4 or 8 directions), `create-character-state` (4 or 8). `/characters/animations` is the only route that fans out into one background job per direction (`background_job_ids`), so its output count is directions x frames across jobs.
Bug-relevant: `jobImages()` prefers inline `images` over `storage_urls` (`routes.ts:390-408`), so a route that returns both (create-character does) is fine, but any new route returning `image_url` only is read as one image.

## 3. Unused contract fields that are quality levers

None of these appear in `routes.ts` types, i.e. PixelDoll cannot send them today. All are **V** from the schema.

Palette control (generation-time, exact colors instead of the app's post-hoc snap):

- `target_palette` with `{colors, name, max_colors, smart}` on `edit-animation-v2`, `edit-images-v2` (not typed on `EditImagesV2`), `transfer-outfit-v2`, `create-character-v3` (not typed), `create-character-pro`, `create-character-pro-flash`, `create-character-state`, `CreateCharacterAnimationRequest`. Contract: every pixel of every frame/view is one of `colors`, one choice for all frames; `max_colors` 2-64; `smart` is beta per-object snapping and costs 15-60 s. This is the direct alternative to `standingDyeMatcher`/`paletteDistance` post-processing, which currently cannot reach exact colors (x31: exact share 0 for both item routes, `:86`).
- `color_image` + `force_colors` (create-with-8-directions and `/characters/animations`): documented palette reference input for create-with-8-directions and `/characters/animations`.
- `use_color_palette_from_reference` and `override_frame_size` (32-256, multiple of 4) on `create-character-state`; the latter is the only contract way to give a state (a big weapon, wings) a larger canvas than the tight source.

Style and prompt steering (create routes):

- `shading` (`flat`/`basic`/`medium`/`detailed`), `outline` (`lineless`, `selective outline`, ...), `detail` (`low`/`medium`/`high`), `text_guidance_scale` (default 8), `proportions`, `isometric` on `create-character-with-8-directions` only. `create-character-v3` has `outline`/`detail` typed but `shading` and `text_guidance_scale` are absent from that route.
- `enhance_prompt` on `create-character-v3`, `CreateCharacterAnimationRequest` and `/animate-with-text-v3` (+0.05 gen each) and on `create-character-pixen`; PixelDoll calls the separate enhance endpoints instead, which costs the same but is a second round trip.
- `create-character-pro`: `style_character_id` (an existing 8-direction character as the style reference for every direction), `style_description`, `method: rotate_character`, `reference_image` (<=168).
- `create-character-pro-flash`: `style_image` with `usage_description`, `style_options {color_palette, outline, detail, shading}`, `first_frame_direction`, `source_image_id` (reuse a paid south image without paying it again).

Motion and stability levers:

- `drift_threshold` on `/animate-with-text-v3` and `/animate-pixminimax`: color de-flicker against frame 0 (0 corrects every frame). Directly targets the "exact color differs on about 70 % of pixels" finding (x31:85) and is not in `TextAnimation` (`routes.ts:315-322`).
- `keep_first_frame: false` is typed; `custom_start_frame` and `end_frame` (max 256, one direction only, enables interpolation) are not. `end_frame` is the only contract way to pin the last pose of a clip.
- `animation_group_id` on `/characters/animations`: extend an existing animation with more directions; a duplicate direction is rejected with 409 instead of being billed twice. Guard against double-spend and free view retrieval.
- `animate-with-skeleton-v3`: `template_id` (mannequin vs quadruped changes joint depth), `view`, and the full 18-joint `first_frame_keypoints`/`keypoints` with `z_index`. The contract warns that a skeleton not matching the image "quietly costs you transparency and colour accuracy" - i.e. partial keypoints are a quality risk, not a no-op.
- `inpaint-v3`: `crop_to_mask` (default true, clean edges) and the 1024 context image, both unused. `edit-image-pro-flash`: `use_color_palette_correction`.
- `edit-images-v2` `method: edit_with_reference` with a 512 px `reference_image` is typed only through `editImageSheet`; the contract's 15/8/3 frame caps mean one slot is spent on the reference, which is why the x31 sheets used text mode with the reference as a cell.

## 4. What is unverified

- `/transfer-outfit-v2` pricing: no usage record at any size. R5 prices it at 10 for 6 frames (`R5:40`), which assumes the mean-side bands hold; the 15/8/3 caps are contract text only.
- `/create-character-state` pricing per direction, and whether it bills 4/8 rotations as one call or per view.
- `/create-character-pro-flash` price: only `/pro-flash/cost` and its itemized cost stages; the "5 image units" figure belongs to `/create-image-pro-flash`, not this route.
- `/animate-with-text-v3` price formula (budget documented, price not).
- Whether `edit-images-v2`/`inpaint-v3` price by mean side or by sqrt(area): 392x284 (mean 338) billed 15 supports both; the exact 15/25 boundary sits anywhere in mean side [336, 360) (`items.ts:226-232`).
- `create-character-with-8-directions` standard-mode canvas growth (>=128 requested) and whether the 1-gen price survives a large `image_size`.
- All 15/8/3 transfer and 16/9/4 edit caps are contract text; only the 4-frame cap above 80 px has a live refusal record (`:38`).
- Per-route USD: `usage.usd` is not recorded for most calls, so USD figures are gen x 0.005 (D).

## 5. Implications for the spike (one line each)

1. Canvas-priced routes reward packing; the 512 px ceiling of `/edit-images-v2` is the only headroom above 256 and it is 2x cheaper per view than anchored at 92 px (15 vs 60 for 3 views x 6 frames).
2. `target_palette` (exact `colors`) is the untested path to the palette consistency that post-hoc snapping cannot reach, at no documented extra charge.
3. `drift_threshold` and `end_frame` are the only contract levers on within-clip flicker and pose endpoints; both are absent from `routes.ts` and untested by PixelDoll.
