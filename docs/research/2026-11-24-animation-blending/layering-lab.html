What the demo cannot prove

One body, one outfit, one punch, south only, no long coat, no skirt, no two-handed weapon, and no real engine rendering. A cape already fails: its lower half would have to be both behind and in front of the legs. Those are the reasons the next step is a review gate on real characters, not a promise.
