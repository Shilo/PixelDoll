Putting walking legs under a punch pose

Both panels use the real demo character. The red pixels are what the attack picture loses. The green pixels are what is brought in from the walk picture.

1. One straight cut through the whole pose this is what breaks

A single fixed line. Everything above the line comes from the attack, everything below from the walk. Drag the line and watch the fists, forearms and any held weapon drop out.

2. Remove only what is attached to the feet what I mean

The attack keeps its whole upper body, including fists, sleeves and a held sword. Only pixels connected to the feet are erased, then the walk's legs are placed under the hips and shifted to line up. Nothing is cut across the arms.

3. Both halves must face the same way

Removing the legs works because the two pictures share a viewpoint. A front-facing chest on legs drawn from behind does not join, no matter how well it is aligned.

works south punch + south walk
fails south punch + north walk

South plus south joins. South plus north puts a face on backwards legs. This is why the chest cannot aim somewhere the legs are not facing without newly drawn art or new sideways/backward walk cycles.

Demo fixture only: plain shirt and shorts, no cape or long coat. Items that hang across the join (cape, coat, low-held weapon) are the cases that still need a decision, because the same pixels would have to be both in front of and behind the legs.
