# Research: purposeful identity and generic AI interface patterns

Date: November 7, 2026. Independent research and challenge for the [Bronze UI identity audit](bronze-ui-identity-audit-2026-11-07.md). This note proposes evaluation criteria; it does not approve a visual redesign or supersede the [production ownership contract](../design/production-experience.md).

## Finding

The concern is credible as a perception of the finished product. Similar rounded controls, low-contrast charcoal surfaces, muted sans-serif labels and lightly tinted selected cards can accumulate into an anonymous tool aesthetic. A technically custom theme can still feel familiar. The user's coworker independently identified colors, matte surfaces and typography, and asked for more intentional UI art. That is useful qualitative feedback from one viewer, not a representative usability study or proof of AI authorship.

There is no defensible universal list of AI colors, fonts or corner radii in the sources reviewed. The productive response is to make authorship visible through coherent choices tied to this product: character-centered composition, readable equipment choices, exact palette work, clear paid actions and a convincing relationship between the world, artwork and controls. Deliberately inconvenient controls would undermine that purpose even if they looked unusual.

## Evidence and limits

All sources below were consulted on November 7, 2026. Summaries distinguish direct observations from implications for PixelDoll.

| Source | What it supports | Limit and application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Anthropic, Improving frontend design through Skills](https://claude.com/resources/articles/improving-frontend-design-through-skills), November 12, 2025 | The model vendor describes convergence toward familiar fonts, purple gradients, standard layouts and minimal animation when prompts lack direction. It also reports reconvergence on another font after steering away from initial defaults. | Vendor examples and prompting guidance, not a controlled cross-model prevalence study. Concrete art direction is useful; copying its font blacklist or RPG recipe could produce another preset. |
| [Doshi and Hauser, Science Advances](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290), 2024 | Their preregistered short-story experiment found individual creative gains alongside reduced collective output diversity with AI assistance. | A writing study cannot establish which UI shapes are overused or diagnose PixelDoll. It motivates independent references and criticism rather than relying on one model's first suggestions. Publisher abstract/search record consulted; the PMC full-page fetch presented a browser check. |
| [Ashkinaze et al., How AI Ideas Affect the Creativity, Diversity, and Evolution of Human Ideas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13481), revised July 31, 2025; accepted at ACM Collective Intelligence 2025 | In their different, dynamic ideation experiment, high AI exposure increased collective diversity without improving individual creativity. | The direction of diversity effects depends on task and process. Avoid presenting inevitable homogenization as a settled law. Abstract consulted. |
| [Google, Usability Hasn't Peaked](https://research.google/pubs/usability-hasnt-peaked-exploring-how-expressive-design-overcomes-the-usability-plateau/), CHI 2026 | Across 48 participants and 10 apps, the authors report faster target fixation, faster task completion and improved ratings for their expressive versions. | This validates investigation of meaningful emphasis and grouping, not every expressive treatment or a prediction of the same gains in PixelDoll. Published abstract consulted. |
| [Google Design, Expressive Design research](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research) | Google describes color, shape, size, motion and containment as ways to communicate function and direct attention. | A system creator's research account. Do not transplant Material's visual identity or add unrelated shapes simply because the system is researched. |
| [NN/g, Flat UI Elements Attract Less Attention and Cause Uncertainty](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/flat-ui-less-attention-cause-uncertainty/), September 3, 2017 | An eye-tracking study compared matched pages with different clickability cues. Weak cues increased time spent locating the target. | The reported 22% increase concerns targeted findability, not all end-to-end use. Flat appearance and unclear interaction are different problems. Readable labels, borders, selection and focus can supply cues without glossy decoration. |
| [Rasmus Andersson, The Inter typeface project](https://rsms.me/work/inter/) | The type designer describes deliberate work for screen-based functional typography. | Designer intent is not comparative evidence that a specific face is best here. It is a strong counterexample to treating a familiar font as inherently lazy or AI-made. |
| [W3C, Understanding Non-text Contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html) | Meaningful identifying information for authored controls and states needs 3:1 contrast against adjacent colors, with stated exceptions. | Decorative rims and every control outline are not automatically required. Evaluate the cue that actually identifies a control or its state; an aesthetic opinion is not a contrast audit. |

## Patterns to challenge, without turning them into bans

These are review hypotheses and practical failure modes, not measured frequencies across AI products.

| Pattern | Why it can feel generic or fail | Purposeful exception and PixelDoll response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An accent pasted onto a charcoal component library | Changing a highlight color leaves composition, tonal hierarchy and control rhythm anonymous. | Warm Bronze remains useful for primary actions and selected states. Test whether selected, focused, hovered and ordinary controls remain distinct when seen together, rather than changing the hue to escape a trend. |
| Every grouping becomes a rounded bordered card | Repeated containment adds visual noise and makes unrelated things feel equally important. | Use a card for a selectable specimen or bounded task. Keep the world open and let spacing or typography group content that needs no separate enclosure. |
| Identical emphasis across all controls | A wall of equally bright borders or accent fills makes the next action harder to find. | Clear field boundaries remain necessary. Compare the real creator form and the primary paid action at ordinary viewing size, including disabled and invalid states. |
| All surfaces feel like the same matte slab | The floating control layer and the work area lose a meaningful relationship to the scene. | Opaque work surfaces support reading; they should stay quiet. Test a restrained material cue where it explains elevation or interaction, preserving solid fallbacks and dense-form readability. Texture everywhere is another indiscriminate treatment. |
| A dramatic background carries all identity | A screenshot can feel atmospheric while its menus and forms still feel unrelated. | Preserve the actor's prominence. Give the actual equipment, size diagram and direction controls appropriate visual care; compare overlays independently as well as in the scene. |
| An unfamiliar font is used as proof of craft | Decorative glyphs, unfamiliar numerals or poor spacing can make a working tool harder to read. | Keep a functional UI face when it performs well. Study hierarchy, weight, size and spacing before replacing it. Restrict expressive type to roles where it supports the intended feeling. |
| Ornament is added in reaction to criticism | Rivets, glow, parchment, grain or pixel borders can create another stock game preset and reduce clarity. | Develop a small coherent art vocabulary from the actual dressing task and existing world. Test one intervention at a time, including a version retaining current geometry. |
| Polish is demonstrated only in a hero screenshot | Loading, empty libraries, long labels, errors and touch layouts expose unfinished decisions. | Review real states at equal dimensions in both schemes. Craft includes recovery, control response and consistent details, not only imagery. |

Anthropic's own example of an RPG direction uses ornamental frames, old-world materials and serif display type. That illustrates why an instruction as broad as “make it game-like” is insufficient: it can merely select another familiar model recipe. The same article's admission that alternative typography can reconverge supports keeping the decision process more specific than a list of approved fashionable fonts. These are reasons to challenge the recipe, not to reject serif type or material detail.

## Challenge to the lead's initial interpretation

The lead reports that Pixel Studio's captured app has a lavender accent, persistent sidebar, bordered field panels and a utility workbench; PixelDoll has warm Bronze, open scenery, a larger central actor, Georgia display headings and contextual equipment. Those are substantial differences. The similar small controls do not establish that either app was generated with AI, and screenshot similarity does not establish copied code or shared CSS.

I agree that the creator's dense controls are the stronger comparison than the Characters scene. I would not make the background/sprite mismatch the sole explanation: the coworker explicitly mentioned the controls' palette, flatness and font. Evaluate scene cohesion and control craftsmanship separately so a new background does not become a substitute for addressing the reported issue. Conversely, changing every control could damage a successful immersive composition.

The current 8/12/16 radius family is a coherent grouping convention. No reviewed source establishes those exact values as optimal, or as an AI fingerprint. Keep it as the baseline. Compare an alternative only if there is a concrete hypothesis about fit, touch response, grouping or visual weight. Rounded corners do not excuse poor contrast, and square corners do not demonstrate human authorship.

## Evaluation proposal for later authorized theme work

Write a brief decision record in the existing plan or review: intended user task and feeling; observed problem; candidate change; why it helps; strongest alternative including retaining the baseline; tradeoff; evidence and confidence. A small correction needs proportionate explanation, not a large design ceremony. Choose which decisions matter to the hypothesis rather than changing the whole palette, face, shapes and background together.

For the first proof, retain the current composition and compare one restrained material/affordance study in Bronze with the baseline. Include the actual creator, an equipment category, a small popover and Export, with default, selected, hovered, keyboard-focused, disabled and error states as applicable. Include dark/light and portrait/landscape. Test whether a newcomer can identify the selected item, the next action, and a generation cost, then ask which version feels cohesive and specific to character dressing and why. Record misses and comments rather than using an “AI detector” score. A small formative sample can reveal problems; it cannot establish population-level preference.

An artwork study can be a separate comparison, informed by the existing [pixel-art world reference](../design/pixel-art-worlds-reference.html). Keep the actor, wording and control layout constant when testing that hypothesis. Do not add fictional stats, rarity, game currencies, heavy framing, sound or motion merely to signal game identity. Preserve the existing simplicity-first, accessibility, performance and paid-request boundaries.

Shared appearance belongs in Bronze UI; PixelDoll owns task composition and domain artwork. The research author changed no appearance, installed no dependency, downloaded no reference application and made no paid requests. Browser observations and verification belong to the companion audit; this independent note is source research and a challenge to its interpretation.
